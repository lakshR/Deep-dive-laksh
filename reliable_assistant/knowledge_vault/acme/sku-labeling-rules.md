# SKU Labeling Rules
Every product carries a SKU in the canonical form SKU- followed by three to six digits, for example SKU-099.
Labels must be printed with a 128 barcode and placed on the top-right face of the carton. Damaged labels are replaced
by the receiving clerk before the carton moves to the pick area.
