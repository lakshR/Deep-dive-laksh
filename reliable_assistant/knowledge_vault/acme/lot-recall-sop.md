# Lot Recall SOP
When a quality hold is placed on LOT-88392, warehouse staff must quarantine all remaining units within 4 hours and
tag the bin with a red hold label. The shift supervisor records the quarantine in the inventory system and notifies
Quality Assurance. Released stock may only ship after QA signs the release form. See [[sku-labeling-rules]].
